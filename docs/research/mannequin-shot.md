# Plain-rig pose for an illustrated shot

Read 2026-09-20 (runtime UTC clock reports 2026-09-19).
Stan Prokopenko, **How to Draw Gesture**, public written Lesson Notes:
https://www.proko.com/course-lesson/how-to-draw-gesture

The notes distinguish movement between masses from copying their contours,
prioritize the long action line, and discuss alternating/asymmetric head, rib,
pelvis and leg rhythms. This informed a distinct waist bend, opposing chest
rotation, and tilted head rather than adding armor detail. Only the accessible
written notes were used, not premium material or a claim to have watched video.

Adaptation: pose the existing plain skeleton in a fixed shot, with dimensions
separate from reach targets and material. Manual reference inspection supports
only the screen relationships; hidden joint depths remain artistic hypotheses.
