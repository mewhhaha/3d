## Reusable capability

Named face-domain construction regions can now survive a topology-changing operation when that operation knows the exact source-face provenance of its generated faces. `remapFaceRegions()` accepts a target-face → source-face mapping plus optional target-only structural regions. `solidifyGeometry()` uses that mapping internally, preserving source semantic names on outer, inner and rim faces and optionally adding `<prefix>.outer`, `<prefix>.inner`, and `<prefix>.rim` roles.

This replaces a previously awkward failure mode: a tagged sheet became stale as soon as solidification changed triangle count, forcing authors either to retag generated topology or to approximate correspondence with closest-surface queries. Exact known construction lineage now stays exact; proximity remains a separate tool for genuinely unrelated topology.

## Research and implementation decision

- Blender 4.5 LTS Manual, Extrude Mesh Node: https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/4.5/modeling/geometry_nodes/mesh/operations/extrude_mesh.html — documents distinct generated Top/Side selections and domain-aware attribute propagation. Adaptation: topology helpers should expose structural output roles and use known source correspondence instead of requiring post-hoc proximity.
- Blender 4.5 LTS Manual, Attributes: https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/4.5/modeling/geometry_nodes/attributes_reference.html — attribute domain is part of meaning, and interpolation through geometry changes is distinct from applying data to unrelated geometry. Adaptation: these remain face-domain construction semantics and exact provenance is kept separate from closest-surface transfer.
- Full bounded notes: `docs/research/face-region-remap.md`.

## Examples and visual evidence

An organic swept leaf preserves `leaf.tip` through a smooth-rim shell, intersects it with `leaf.shell.outer` and `leaf.shell.rim`, and uses the inherited outer-tip region to place a bud with the spatial-query API. A deformed hard-surface panel preserves `panel.service` and `panel.mount` through sharp-rim solidification, highlights service/outer and mount/rim intersections, and constrains a socket to the inherited service exterior.

## Limits and next workflow target

The generic remapper uses an explicit mapping callback and an “any parent belongs” rule when a target face has multiple source parents. It does not retain a persistent lineage graph, transfer point/edge/face-corner semantics, infer correspondence through arbitrary booleans/remeshing, or export authoring regions as glTF semantics. `solidifyGeometry()` remains a vertex-normal offset shell and can self-intersect in tight concavities.

The next workflow priority is to reuse exact face provenance in another bounded topology constructor—preferably profile-sweep caps/side roles or an explicitly owned geometry merge—so semantic regions can survive multi-stage construction without turning `remapFaceRegions()` into a universal remeshing abstraction.
