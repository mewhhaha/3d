# Workflow checkpoint — exact persistent-anchor remap through owned composition

Implementation revision: `97cb5311bd4f8cc36cfa17a0b25ba743f596a1b7`.
Capability: keep an authored barycentric attachment exact when independently editable source parts are transformed and merged by `composeGeometries()`.

## Accepted change

`composeGeometries()` now records every source part's indexed-topology signature alongside its deterministic vertex and face ranges. `remapCompositionAnchor()` accepts a bind-created persistent anchor plus an explicit part name, validates that the anchor belongs to the recorded source topology, offsets its face and corner indices into the combined topology, verifies those target corner indices, and reuses the generic `remapSurfaceAnchor()` contract. Because composition preserves source triangle corner order, barycentric coordinates and affine tangent weights do not need permutation.

Part identity is intentionally explicit. Repeated components may share the exact same topology signature, so topology alone is insufficient to decide whether an anchor means the left, center or right copy. After remap, the combined topology owns the returned anchor and normal same-topology anchor evaluation applies.

- Three.js r186 `BufferGeometry` documents ordered index triples and whole-geometry matrix transforms. Adaptation: use the repository constructor's tested deterministic vertex/face offsets, not post-merge geometric search.
- Three.js r186 `Matrix4` documents composed transforms. Adaptation: verify that a transformed/non-uniformly scaled part's remapped support origin matches the source barycentric origin transformed by the same matrix within `1e-6` m.
- Blender 4.5 LTS `Transform Geometry` documents applying a transform to whole geometry in local space. Adaptation: keep bind intent in source-part local authoring space and remap after the known transform/merge stage.
- Blender 5.3 development `Join Geometry` documents offset-style input indices. It is only a version-specific precedent, not a compatibility guarantee; this implementation validates its own exact target corner indices.

## Ownership and limitations

This is exact constructor provenance, not a scene dependency graph, Boolean/weld operation, or semantic remesher. The composition contract still requires compatible ordinary point attributes and rejects interleaved data, tangents, skin weights and morph targets. The helper cannot infer which repeated copy an anchor belongs to without the explicit part name. Later topology changes need another operation-specific remap or a deliberate rebind/nearest-surface decision. Anchors remain authoring metadata and are not glTF semantics.

## Rejected approach

Do not choose a target part by nearest surface or topology signature when the constructor already has a stable authored part name and exact offsets. Do not silently accept a bind-created anchor whose topology signature differs from the source part metadata. Do not claim transform correctness from a render alone: the unit test compares the exact transformed support origin and target corner indices.
