# Workflow checkpoint — reusable local deformation handles

Base remote revision: `be61e4c0853e73b18de40809d9dd05bd21225e34`.
Implementation revision: `19b99601a2c241b342b4e7800c383577f07a06db`.
Capability: make broad pre-rig primary-form edits on ordinary indexed `BufferGeometry` through reusable geometry-local bend, twist and taper handles whose placement stays independent of point-domain selection masks and mesh topology.

## Accepted change

`src/lib/geometry-deform.js` adds `deformationHandle()`, `bendVertices()`, `twistVertices()`, `taperVertices()` and `deformGeometry()`.

A handle owns geometry-local translation, repository-standard XYZ-degree rotation, positive scale and one finite interval along handle-local +Y. Operations remain separate from selection intent: the existing point-domain masks from `geometry-sculpt.js`, including semantic face-region selections, can control influence without becoming part of the deformation math. Operations compose sequentially, so one compact construction can taper, bend and twist a primary form while later authors remain free to move the handle or replace the selection.

Bend maps the handle centerline to a circular arc and continues points beyond the bounded range rigidly along the end tangent. Twist rotates the local X/Z cross-section around +Y, and taper scales that cross-section linearly while rejecting collapse/reflection. Fractional selection weights blend the current point toward the fully deformed result. The source geometry is cloned; topology, UVs, ordinary custom attributes and named face-region metadata remain owned by the result, while normals/bounds and existing tangents are rebuilt. Skin attributes and morph targets are rejected rather than silently treated as valid after rest-position edits.

Existing sculpt operations are unchanged. This is a broad-form stage that complements radial/path/projected masks and smooth/relax cleanup instead of replacing them.

## Research used

See `docs/research/geometry-deformation-handles.md`.

- Alan H. Barr, *Global and local deformations of solid primitives* (SIGGRAPH 1984): bend, twist and taper are useful position-dependent modeling transforms that can compose hierarchically. Adaptation: a small analytic pre-rig vocabulary with explicit authoring frames, not a free-form lattice or elasticity system.
- Blender Manual, Simple Deform Modifier: local-coordinate deformation separates origin/orientation, deformation axis/range and vertex-group influence. Adaptation: keep frame/range and point-domain selection independent; v1 fixes the deform axis to local +Y and rotates the frame instead of reproducing every Blender axis/lock/stretch option.
- Three.js r186 `Matrix4`: `compose()` and `invert()` provide the locked dependency primitives used to move positions into and out of the authoring frame. Positive handle scale keeps that inverse well-defined.

## Remaining limitations / next workflow priority

This is a compact analytic deformation layer, not Blender Simple Deform, FFD/lattice deformation, elasticity or collision-aware bending. V1 has one local +Y axis, one circular bend model, no Stretch mode, no exact volume preservation, no self-intersection prevention and no rig/morph refit. Strong bends remain tessellation-dependent, and soft selections interpolate positions rather than solving a physically smooth transition.

The next general workflow priority is **curve-guided broad deformation with transported local frames**: let an authored centerline, including S-curves and non-circular arcs, drive an existing mesh cross-section while preserving the same independent selection and topology/attribute ownership contract. Exercise that on an organic appendage and an unrelated routed hard-surface member before considering a larger FFD system. No cyber-android likeness change is claimed in this pass.
