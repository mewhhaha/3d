## Reusable capability

Semantic construction intent can now be stored as named face-domain regions rather than overloaded onto Three.js material groups. `defineFaceRegions()` resolves predicates or explicit triangle lists once and stores JSON-safe names plus compressed triangle ranges. Regions may overlap, survive `BufferGeometry.clone()`, and deliberately fail as stale when triangle count changes.

The same names now drive multiple authoring systems: `triangleSpatialIndex().closestPoint()` accepts `regionNames` / `regionMatch`; `transferSurfaceAttributes()` accepts `sourceRegions` / `sourceRegionMatch`; and `faceRegionVertexMask()` explicitly converts face ownership into point-domain editing weights.

## Research and implementation decision

- Blender 4.5 Attributes manual: attribute domain is semantic; face, point, edge and face-corner ownership are distinct. Adaptation: regions are explicitly face-domain and face-to-point conversion is a separate operation.
- Blender 4.5 Visibility, Masking & Face Sets: face sets group faces and can feed masks. Adaptation: a named region can feed both correspondence and point-mask creation without claiming Blender's interactive face-set feature set.
- Three.js r186 BufferGeometry: groups are draw/material partitions and cannot overlap, while JSON-safe `userData` is cloneable metadata. Decision: rejected encoding semantic regions as `BufferGeometry.groups`; resolved region metadata lives in `geometry.userData` instead.
- Full bounded notes: `docs/research/face-regions.md`.

## Limits and next workflow target

Named regions are topology-dependent construction metadata, not glTF semantics. Arbitrary merges, booleans and remeshes do not yet transfer region ownership, and the triangle spatial index still snapshots immutable source triangles. The next workflow target is explicit region remapping through a topology-changing operation with a known source/target relationship—preferably solidification or profile rebuilding—rather than pretending to solve universal semantic remeshing.
