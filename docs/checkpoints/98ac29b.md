Capability: keep an independently editable component attached to an authored named support surface, with explicit support-local search intent, clearance, orientation and local edit transform that can be re-resolved after the support shape changes.

## Accepted change

`src/lib/surface-mount.js` adds `surfaceMount()`, `resolveSurfaceMount()` and `attachSurfaceMount()`. A mount is JSON-safe construction data: support-local `near`, optional named face-region filters and facing constraint, smooth/face normal mode, a support-local tangent hint, normal offset, and an editable local position/rotation/scale. Resolution reuses the existing triangle spatial index, builds a tangent/bitangent/normal frame, then post-multiplies the author's local transform. The support mesh is never mutated, and a prebuilt spatial index may be reused for multiple mounts on one immutable support.

This replaces repeated one-off world transforms for attachments. Shape intent stays in the support, component geometry remains independent, and changing support curvature can re-evaluate placement without rewriting the component or hiding correction coordinates inside it.

## Ownership and limitations

The mount coordinate contract is deliberately narrow: `near`, tangent hint, query normal and returned matrix are all in support-geometry local space, so support and mounted component normally share an assembly parent. Rebuild the spatial index after support vertices change. Nearest-surface re-resolution can jump to another admissible face after a large shape change; named regions and facing constraints reduce ambiguity but do not create persistent barycentric identity. The helper does not solve collisions, inside/outside, ray/project modes, rig parenting, shear/reflection, or a scene dependency graph. When the tangent hint nearly matches the normal, it uses a deterministic projected triangle-edge fallback rather than transported twist.
