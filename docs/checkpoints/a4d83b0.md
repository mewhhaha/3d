Capability: make local primary-form edits on ordinary indexed Three.js geometry using reusable spatial/orientation/semantic masks while preserving topology and ordinary attributes.

## Accepted change

`src/lib/geometry-sculpt.js` adds a small point-domain selection algebra for ordinary indexed `BufferGeometry`: `radialSelection()`, `facingSelection()`, `faceRegionSelection()`, `intersectSelections()`, `unionSelections()`, `invertSelection()` and `selectionWeights()`. The first brush operations are `pullVertices()`, `inflateVertices()` and `smoothVertices()`, evaluated by `sculptGeometry()` in geometry-local meter space.

The operation clones its input, preserves its index and ordinary UV/color/custom attributes, retains named face-region metadata because topology is unchanged, and recomputes normals/bounds. Existing tangents are rebuilt when indexed position/normal/UV data are available. Skin attributes and morph targets are rejected because this operation does not own a rig or morph refit. It is a topology-preserving pre-rig form-edit stage, not a remesher, Boolean system or deformation graph.

This is intentionally separate from `src/lib/forms/sculpt.js`: cage sculpt owns the repository's shared-quad construction topology before compilation, while this operation serves arbitrary ordinary indexed triangle geometry that has already reached the Three.js `BufferGeometry` layer.

## Research used

See `docs/research/geometry-sculpt-masks.md`.

- Blender 4.5 LTS Visibility, Masking & Face Sets: continuous point masks and face-domain grouping are distinct concepts. Adaptation: convert existing named face regions explicitly to point weights, then compose them with other selectors rather than overloading render groups or vertex IDs.
- Blender sculpt brush falloff documentation: spatial influence and normal/front-facing influence are separate author controls. Adaptation: keep `radialSelection()` and `facingSelection()` independent and combine them with mask algebra.
- Blender Smooth / Smooth Vertices documentation: positional smoothing has strength/repeat semantics and is distinct from shading-normal smoothing. Adaptation: use bounded simultaneous one-ring averaging, with open boundaries pinned by default, then rebuild shading normals afterward.
- Three.js `BufferGeometry`: cloned geometry can preserve attributes/index while edited positions require updated normals/bounds; tangent rebuilding requires indexed position/normal/UV. Adaptation: clone ownership and explicit dependent-data handling rather than mutating the source.

## Rejected / bounded behavior

Do not silently retain skin weights or morph deltas after changing rest positions; v1 rejects those owners instead of implying a correct refit. Do not convert face-region semantics into permanent raw vertex IDs; the region-to-point conversion remains explicit and fractional on shared boundaries. Do not add dynamic remeshing or collision behavior to this bounded topology-preserving layer. Three.js tangent rebuilding is mechanically supported but is not a substitute for the repository's MikkTSpace normal-bake path when production tangent-space normal maps require it.

## Remaining limitations / next workflow priority

Selectors currently cover radial distance, normal facing and named face regions in geometry-local coordinates. Smoothing is simple mesh-density-dependent Laplacian averaging and can shrink volume; pull/inflate can self-intersect. There is no stroke/path selection, geodesic support, projected/screen brush, pressure input, symmetry helper for this layer, or rig/morph refit.

The next general workflow priority is **geometry-local stroke/path selection plus symmetry/local-frame composition for ordinary `BufferGeometry`**. That would make grooves, ridges, seams, creature folds and hard-surface panel edits expressible as reusable paths instead of chains of hand-placed radial masks, while keeping the same topology/attribute ownership contract. No cyber-android likeness change is claimed in this pass.
