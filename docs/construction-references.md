## Project worklist, not a tutorial summary

The current pose study remains the baseline. The existing render's facial features sit on a broad, minimally shaped face, while body armor is still dominated by simple repeated sections. Better placement has not solved the head/hair outline or primary body forms. These are observations of our rendered checkpoint, not conclusions extracted from the videos.

For the next geometry pass:

- **Body construction study:** retain the existing guide, camera and equal limb lengths. Work in neutral clay on the ribcage/pelvis relationship, shoulder mass, taper of the upper/lower limbs and extremity volumes. Compare front, side, back and hero views. Keep the anatomical blockout independent of shell panels so a volume correction does not require replacing the pose.
- **Head construction study:** show skull, jaw and neck first; then brow/eye-socket, cheek, nasal and muzzle/chin planes. Establish the profile as well as the frontal placement before colored eye overlays, lashes or hair conceal the structure. Keep the existing face as a baseline until the replacement is visually reviewed.
- **API design:** reuse compact masks, `pull`, `inflate`, `flatten` and `relax` on shared geometry. Add named construction regions and saved intermediate stages only when an actual sculpt needs them. Treat symmetry, connected/remeshed primary volumes and later edge-loop retopology as separate capabilities, not a misleading all-in-one `human()` function.
- **Detail and delivery:** do not try to fix an incorrect primary shape with a normal texture. Delay high-only surface detail until the low-resolution form passes the reference and alternative-view review. Record topology/UV changes explicitly; a voxel remesh would invalidate previous UV and high/low correspondence unless a new transfer step is implemented.

These are planned work items. This reference-intake checkpoint implements no new anatomy, remeshing, face topology or character deformation. It does not claim the linked tutorials' techniques have been reproduced. Existing geometry code and models are unchanged.
